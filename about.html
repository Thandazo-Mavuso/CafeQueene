Our Story

Meet the Owner

Started by Queene Mavuso in 2025.

Café Queene was born out of a simple desire: to create a space that feels less like a rushed corporate coffee counter and more like an extension of your own living room. Founded by Queene Mavuso, our doors opened in 2025 with the goal of serving exceptional, everyday coffee alongside a reliable, comforting breakfast and lunch menu.

Whether you are a student looking for a quiet booth to study, a remote worker needing steady Wi-Fi and a strong flat white, or friends meeting up for a long lunch, Café Queene is designed for you to come as you are and stay as long as you like.
