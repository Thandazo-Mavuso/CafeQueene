About Café Queene

Café Queene is a new coffee shop that started in 2025. The business is owned by Queene Mavuso.

Café Queene offers good coffee as well as breakfast and lunch. Our goal is to provide a comfortable and welcoming environment where customers can relax, eat and enjoy their coffee.

Our Story

Café Queene was created to provide customers with a modern and trendy coffee shop experience. We want to create a place where students, young professionals and people working remotely can spend time comfortably.

Café Queene currently has two branches, one in Middelburg and another in Nelspruit.

Our Goal

Our goal is to introduce people to Café Queene, provide quality food and coffee, and build a community of regular customers.
